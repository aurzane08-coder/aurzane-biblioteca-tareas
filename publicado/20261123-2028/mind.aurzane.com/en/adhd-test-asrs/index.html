About this test

ASRS v1.1 is a validated, widely used questionnaire. Scores range from 0 to 6. 0 to 3: not typical of adult adhd; 4 to 6: highly consistent with adult adhd.

Reference: Kessler RC, Adler L, Ames M, et al. The World Health Organization Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS): a short screening scale for use in the general population. Psychol Med. 2005;35(2):245-256. © World Health Organization. Score: number of answers in the shaded zone (Sometimes or more for questions 1 to 3; Often or more for questions 4 to 6).
