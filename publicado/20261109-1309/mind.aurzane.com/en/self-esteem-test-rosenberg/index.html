About this test

RSES is a validated, widely used questionnaire. Scores range from 0 to 30. 0 to 14: low self-esteem; 15 to 25: self-esteem in the normal range; 26 to 30: high self-esteem.

Reference: Rosenberg M. Society and the Adolescent Self-Image. Princeton University Press; 1965. The scale is free to use for research and education.
