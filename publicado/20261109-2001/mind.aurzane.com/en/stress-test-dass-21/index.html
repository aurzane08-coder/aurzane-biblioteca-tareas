About this test

DASS-21 (stress) is a validated, widely used questionnaire. Scores range from 0 to 42. 0 to 14: normal stress; 15 to 18: mild stress; 19 to 25: moderate stress; 26 to 33: severe stress; 34 to 42: extremely severe stress.

Reference: Lovibond SH, Lovibond PF. Manual for the Depression Anxiety Stress Scales. 2nd ed. Sydney: Psychology Foundation; 1995. The DASS is in the public domain.
