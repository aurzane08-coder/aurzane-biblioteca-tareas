About this test

SWLS is a validated, widely used questionnaire. Scores range from 5 to 35. 5 to 9: extremely dissatisfied; 10 to 14: dissatisfied; 15 to 19: slightly below average; 20 to 20: neutral; 21 to 25: slightly satisfied; 26 to 30: satisfied; 31 to 35: extremely satisfied.

Reference: Diener E, Emmons RA, Larsen RJ, Griffin S. The Satisfaction With Life Scale. J Pers Assess. 1985;49(1):71-75. Free to use with citation.
