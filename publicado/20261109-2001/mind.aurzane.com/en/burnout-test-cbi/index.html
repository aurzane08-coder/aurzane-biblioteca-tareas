About this test

CBI (work-related) is a validated, widely used questionnaire. Scores range from 0 to 100. 0 to 49.99: no burnout; 50 to 74.99: moderate burnout; 75 to 99.99: high burnout; 100 to 100: severe burnout.

Reference: Kristensen TS, Borritz M, Villadsen E, Christensen KB. The Copenhagen Burnout Inventory: a new tool for the assessment of burnout. Work & Stress. 2005;19(3):192-207. Free to use.
