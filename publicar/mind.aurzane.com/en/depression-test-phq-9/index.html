About this test

PHQ-9 is a validated, widely used questionnaire. Scores range from 0 to 27. 0 to 4: minimal or no depression; 5 to 9: mild depression; 10 to 14: moderate depression; 15 to 19: moderately severe depression; 20 to 27: severe depression.

Reference: Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JBW. The PHQ-9: validity of a brief depression severity measure. J Gen Intern Med. 2001;16(9):606-613. No permission required to reproduce.
